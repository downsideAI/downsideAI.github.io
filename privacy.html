SIROCCO: strike of the desert

Privacy Policy · last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game SIROCCO: strike of the desert (Android package net.sirocco.game, “the game”) collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by an independent developer (“we”). Contact: qiyipopotube60@gmail.com.

What the game itself stores

The game has no accounts of its own. Your progress (levels, upgrades, fuel, tokens, squads, settings) is stored on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, in your cloud save (below). We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your progress in Play Games Saved Games, so it survives a reinstall or a new phone. Play Games gives the game your player ID and stores the save in your Google account; we cannot see your name, email or other games. Signing in is optional: without it the game works the same, with progress kept on the device only. You can delete the cloud save from the Google Play Games settings at any time.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about your interaction with ads and the app, and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google User Messaging Platform); you can change your choice later from the consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings at any time. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game only which product was bought, so it can be delivered. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives a crash report: the error and where in the game it happened, device model, Android and WebView versions, graphics chip name, and a random installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs. They do not contain your name, contacts or files.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the Google services named above, which act on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies. All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

Children

The game is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete or export; your local progress is removed by uninstalling the game, and your cloud save from the Google Play Games settings. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings or contact Google. For any question, write to qiyipopotube60@gmail.com.

Changes
